IMPORTANT

Verification questions

Does verification show who I voted for?

No. The verification process is designed to confirm that a ballot was recorded without displaying the voter's candidate selections.

What if my reference cannot be found?

First check that the reference was entered exactly as provided. If the problem continues, contact the authorized election support team.

Can I use somebody else's reference?

Verification references should be treated as private election records. Only use the reference issued for your own ballot.

Does blockchain mean the system cannot be hacked?

No system should be described as impossible to hack. ALU-ELECT is designed with multiple security layers, and blockchain or cryptographic records are used as part of the overall integrity and audit architecture.
